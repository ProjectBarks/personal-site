# PRODUCT.md

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
brandonbarker.me is Brandon Barker's personal site: a single-author portfolio,
not an app. The design *is* the product. It is being restructured from a
single-page resume dump into a multi-page site whose front door is a bare
terminal landing, with the resume and personal details kept off the front and
reachable only through navigation.

## Users
- Recruiters and hiring managers skimming for signal in under a minute.
- Fellow engineers who arrived from GitHub, a talk, or a shared link.
- Collaborators and the merely curious who want to see what Brandon builds.
They are technical or technical-adjacent, comfortable with a terminal metaphor,
and impatient with marketing fluff.

## Brand & Tone
- Terminal-native, developer-first. The site reads like a well-kept shell.
- Understated confidence. Lowercase throughout. No exclamation, no hype.
- Dry, precise, tinkerer/inventor voice. Copy states facts, not aspirations.
- Fast. The site should feel instant; weight is a design failure.

## Anti-references (never look like these)
- Generic SaaS/portfolio templates: hero-metric blocks, identical card grids,
  icon+heading+text tiles.
- Purple-on-white gradients, glassmorphism, Inter/Roboto/system-font defaults.
- Corny aspirational tagline copy ("pushing the boundaries", "innovating for
  the future"). Explicitly banned.
- A resume/CV or personal contact details dumped on the front page.

## Strategic Principles
- The terminal aesthetic is the identity. Every page is recognizably the same shell.
- One accent color (cobalt), used sparingly, carries all emphasis.
- Content is JSON-driven (data/resume.json); templates render it, nothing is
  hand-duplicated.
- The front page earns attention in one screen; depth lives one nav hop away.
- Lowercase, monospace, dotted rules, tabular years. Consistency over novelty.
